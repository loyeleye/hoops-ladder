Move Check Recorder

A guided session that films you doing each dribble move while it calls them out, so every rep comes labeled. About 25 minutes. Clips stay on this phone until you save or share them.

Setup
  • Prop the phone upright at about eye height, 6 to 10 feet away, screen facing you.
  • Your whole body in the picture, head to feet, with a little room on each side. Before each block it checks this and tells you out loud to move back, closer, left or right. The check runs on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere. In a small room where your feet won't fit, turn on Close quarters below (or tap it during the line-up): head down to the middle of your thighs is then enough, and each clip's labels say which framing it used.
  • Turn the volume up. It talks you through each block, counts down, then calls each rep.
  • Perfect moves aren't needed. Go at a speed that keeps the move clean.
  • Each block shows its steps first, then counts down from 10. Tap Pause if you need longer. Messed a block up? Tap Redo during the rest.
  • Retreat always means two dribbles back with the same hand. No crossover.
  • Between calls, keep a pound dribble going in whichever hand has the ball.
