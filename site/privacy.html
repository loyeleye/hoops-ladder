Hoops Ladder

Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version. Hoops Ladder keeps everything on your phone. There is no account and no server. The app does not collect, upload, share or sell any of your data, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking.

This policy covers the Hoops Ladder app for Android (package com.ladioyeleye.hoopsladder), made by Ladi Oyeleye, an individual developer ("I" below).

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone, so it can show your progress:

  • Your training history: sessions, drills done, makes and attempts, shooting spots, timings, ratings you give drills, XP and levels.
  • Your settings, such as workout length, shooting hand and how you log reps.
  • Results of form checks: numbers such as joint angles and timing. Not the video or any pictures from it.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. Other apps can't read it.

Camera and videos

When you tap Record or choose a clip on the Form tab, Android lets you record a clip with the camera or pick one from your phone. The app reads that clip on your phone, runs a pose model that is built into the app to find your body position, and keeps only the measurements listed above. The clip itself is not uploaded, shared or saved by the app. The camera is used only while you are recording a clip you asked for.

If a future version lets you save training videos in the app, they will be stored only on your phone, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Microphone and voice commands

The app asks for the microphone only if you turn on voice commands (for example, saying "make" or "miss" to log a shot). Voice commands use the speech recognition service on your phone, which is provided by your phone's maker or Google, not by Hoops Ladder. That service may process the audio under its own privacy policy. Hoops Ladder receives only the recognized words, acts on them right away, and doesn't store or send them anywhere. You can use taps, earbuds or a clicker instead and never grant the microphone.

Spoken coaching (the app reading out your score and cues) uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech.

Internet

The app works offline. Everything it needs, including the pose model and the drill animations, ships inside the app. It goes online only when you tap a link to an outside video or article; that link opens in your browser or the YouTube app, and those services' own privacy policies apply there.

Backups

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your phone's backup to your Google account. That backup is controlled by you and Google; I can't see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Sharing and selling

I don't share or sell any data, because the app doesn't collect any. There are no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics, crash reporting or sign-in in the app.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the app has stored, uninstall it, or go to Settings → Apps → Hoops Ladder → Storage → Clear storage. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, that removes all of it.

Children

Hoops Ladder is meant for players aged 13 and up. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, for example if cloud sync or an online coach is added later, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub, or use the developer email address on the app's Google Play page.
